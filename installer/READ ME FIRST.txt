Budget Tracker — test version
=============================

Thanks for trying it out!

STARTING IT
  Double-click "Budget Tracker.exe".

  The first time, Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" window, because
  the app isn't signed with a paid certificate. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

YOUR DATA
  Everything stays on this computer — nothing is sent anywhere.
  - Portable version (the .zip): your data is saved in the "data" folder next to
    Budget Tracker.exe. Keep that folder if you move or update the app.
  - Installed version: your data is saved in %APPDATA%\Budget Tracker\data
    (paste that into the File Explorer address bar to open it). Uninstalling keeps it.
  The start screen shows exactly where your data folder is.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  The app shows a message and saves the details in data\logs\errors.log.
  Please send that file, plus a short note of what you were doing, to whoever gave
  you the app. Screenshots help too.

THINGS WORTH TRYING
  - Create your own user on the start screen.
  - Add a few expenses (PEN or USD), edit one, delete one, then press Ctrl+Z.
  - Set a budget, add a debt with monthly installments, look at the Charts tab.
