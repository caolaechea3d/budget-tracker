## Download

Pick one (Windows 10/11, 64-bit):

- **`BudgetTracker-…-setup.exe`** — installer. Adds a Start-menu shortcut and an uninstaller; no admin rights needed.
- **`BudgetTracker-…-portable.zip`** — no installation. Unzip anywhere (even a USB stick) and run `Budget Tracker.exe`; your data stays in that folder.

The first time, Windows may say **"Windows protected your PC"** because the app isn't code-signed. Click **More info → Run anyway**.

## Your data

Everything stays on your computer. The start screen shows where your data folder is. If something goes wrong, the app saves the details in `data\logs\errors.log` — please send that file along with what you were doing.
